# vibe.md

How bots.ac should *feel*. Product sentiments from Dani, kept as the north star for design and UX decisions. Add new ones at the top with a date.

## 2026-10-16: "Who needs me" is a lively meeting, not a chat inbox

- Answering bots should feel more fulfilling than today's one-chat-at-a-time back-and-forth.
- Picture a lively team meeting or Q&A: Dani is up front, and a room full of agents have their hands up with questions.
- The agents are passionate and eager. Everyone wants to get the job done, and the energy is high.
- Dani answers question after question in a flow, and each answer visibly unblocks someone, who then gets back to work.
- It should feel like leading a great team, not clearing notifications.

### Design notes (to explore)

- A "meeting room" view where agents with questions gather, raise hands and queue up.
- Quick answers (multiple choice with a recommended pick, plus free text) so Dani can move fast.
- Visible momentum: an agent cheers or walks back to its desk once unblocked.
- Never look calm when data is stale (a lesson from Cubicle). Show when updates stop arriving.
